Esports identities and sci-fi event graphics frequently hit the same wall: standard technical sans serifs lack punch, while hyper-stylized display faces turn muddy under harsh screen glare. You're Gone resolves this conflict by pairing blunt geometric mass with razor-sharp stencil incisions. Designed by the Font Bazaar Team across four styles, this all-caps family channels an intense cyber aesthetic that connects classic Y2K gaming optimism to contemporary digital environments.

The letterforms draw their energy from heavy, rounded perimeters sliced apart by modular negative space. These precise cutaways keep massive headline locks readable from across an arena or within a crowded stream overlay. Instead of cold industrial shapes, the softened contours introduce a tactile, playful aggression that suits tournament jerseys, electronic music covers, and collector-edition game packaging.

Delivered in OTF, TTF, WOFF, and WOFF2 formats, the set drops directly into both motion design pipelines and print workflows. The glyph set supports European territories, providing proper diacritics for localized copy in Czech, German, Romanian, and Portuguese. When a project demands sheer structural weight without sacrificing technical clarity, these characters hold their ground under pressure.
