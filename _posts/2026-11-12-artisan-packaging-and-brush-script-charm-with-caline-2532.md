Boutique food and beverage packaging often struggles to balance authentic artisan character with crisp readability on shelf displays. Caline solves this dilemma by introducing a lively brush script crafted specifically for bespoke labels and independent retail goods. Created by the Font Bazaar Team, its rhythmic slant and fluid, hand-drawn strokes bring an approachable personality to small-batch wine bottles, organic honey jars, and specialty bakery boxes.

Its single regular weight maintains clear definition on both textured paper stock and screen mockups, sidestepping the fragile hairpins that often break down in print. Paired with quiet sans-serif text, Caline provides the visual distinction needed for product names, ingredient callouts, and seasonal holiday menus. The design draws on a friendly, romantic spirit that feels distinctly human, steering brands clear of cold, cookie-cutter aesthetics.

Supplied in OTF, TTF, WOFF, and WOFF2 formats, the typeface transitions reliably from physical jar seals to digital storefronts and promotional social media graphics. The character set covers Latin-based typography for projects set in German, Spanish, Portuguese, Tagalog, and Swahili. Caline gives designers a direct route to expressive brush lettering, delivering the warmth of traditional sign-painting to specialty goods without requiring custom hand-lettered illustrations for every layout.
