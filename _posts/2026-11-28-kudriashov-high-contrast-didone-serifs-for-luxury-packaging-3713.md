Designing labels for high-end boutique spirits or artisanal provisions requires letterforms that convey heritage without feeling dated. Kudriashov addresses this tension by marrying sharp Victorian Didone geometry with disciplined neoclassical proportions. Its crisp hairline serifs and severe vertical contrast bring a decisive architectural clarity to glass bottles, printed seals, and bespoke packaging boxes.

Spanning twelve styles across weights from light (300) to bold (700) in normal proportions, the family provides strict typographic hierarchy for complex label suites. Designers can balance dramatic display headlines on an outer gift box against structured ingredient lists and batch notes on a back label. The vertical stress remains taut throughout, preserving legibility across text blocks while demanding visual focus at larger display scales.

Available in OTF, TTF, WOFF, and WOFF2 formats, Kudriashov is tailored for both printed collateral and high-resolution digital storefronts. Its character set covers multiple writing systems, ensuring consistent branding across international markets using languages such as Swahili, Quechua, Zulu, Somali, and English. For studios crafting serious, luxury identities that demand structural precision and historical pedigree, this family delivers uncompromising refinement.
