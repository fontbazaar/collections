Crafting a bespoke bridal identity or a fine fragrance label requires letterforms that feel personal rather than manufactured. Standard digital types often lack the intimate cadence of handwritten ink, leaving luxury packaging feeling cold. Westie Ametta solves this by pairing calligraphic warmth with a disciplined monoline structure, ensuring high legibility at small sizes without losing its romantic fluidity. Fluid ascenders and looped lowercase glyphs create a light, airy rhythm tailored specifically for boutique labels and wedding invitations.

This single-weight script comes in OTF, TTF, WOFF, and WOFF2 formats, ready for both print production and web typography. Designers working on international projects can format texts across multiple regions, as the character set fully supports english, german, spanish, italian, portuguese, and more.
