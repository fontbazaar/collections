Designing title graphics for indie arcade titles and tournament flyers often stalls when standard geometric fonts lack dimensional grit. XRaytid tackles this aesthetic hurdle by injecting jagged outlines and faceted interior shadows directly into a punchy, condensed frame. Its visual rhythm immediately calls to mind late-90s fighting game rosters, rebellious comic book covers, and kinetic Y2K console interfaces where typography needed to feel tangible, dangerous, and loud.

The letterforms maintain a disciplined, space-saving width while unleashing eccentric, segmented geometry across every interior contour. Because the shadow planes are baked directly into the glyph architecture, you achieve a sharp faux-3D illusion without stacking complex vector filters or manual extrusions. That structural bite ensures logos and flyer headlines retain their punch across screen-printed streetwear, novelty carton packaging, and neon digital banners.

XRaytid is available in a single regular weight and ships in OTF, TTF, WOFF, and WOFF2 formats for print and web deployment. It includes character support for languages including English and Zulu. If your layout requires unmistakable counterculture nostalgia instead of clean corporate minimalism, XRaytid gives your headline treatments an authentic retro-gaming bite.
