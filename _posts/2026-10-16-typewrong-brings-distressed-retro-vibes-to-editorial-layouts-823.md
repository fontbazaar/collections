When a nostalgic book cover or indie magazine layout needs an edge that clean digital type simply cannot provide, standard monoline letters often fall flat. Typewrong solves this by injecting an eccentric retro personality into body text and headlines through irregular ink bleeds and a distressed monoline texture. Available in regular and bold weights across two distinct styles, this playful sans serif delivers raw character without sacrificing legibility.

Crafted specifically for designers working on packaging, posters, and editorial spreads, the font bridges the gap between mid-century nostalgia and modern distortion. The character set handles multiple languages including English and Zulu, making it versatile for global branding projects that demand a tactile, imperfect feel. Files are provided in OTF, TTF, WOFF2, and WOFF formats for print and web applications.
