Clean, sterile vector typography often fails when a project calls for the burnt, atmospheric grit of a dark craft brewery label or a gritty horror poster. Carbonized Timber answers that demand with heavily distressed, charred contours that embed raw industrial decay directly into your headline layouts. Built as a display sans serif with mysterious undertones, it eliminates the need to fake distressing through messy raster overlays.

The family includes two styles that capture regular, medium, and bold visual weights, giving designers the flexibility to build layered typographic hierarchies for band merchandise, apparel, and packaging labels. Every glyph appears carved and partially consumed by fire, retaining legibility while projecting a foreboding nature-inspired hostility. The eroded silhouettes hold their crisp bite whether stamped onto textured bottle labels or blown up across full-bleed gig flyers.

Available in OTF, TTF, WOFF, and WOFF2 formats, the typeface adapts smoothly from physical screen prints to digital promotional banners. Its character set accommodates language requirements for English, Quechua, Somali, Swahili, and Zulu. When your visual narrative requires the unapologetic texture of scorched charcoal and weathered earth, this typeface provides the necessary weight.
