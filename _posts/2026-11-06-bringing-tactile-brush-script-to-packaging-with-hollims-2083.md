Artisanal food and beverage packaging often struggles to communicate authentic craftsmanship when typography looks overly computerized. Hollims solves this disconnect by bringing genuine dry-brush texture and lively, slanted handwriting directly to the front of the pack. Developed by the Font Bazaar Team as a single regular-weight script, it injects warmth into label designs that need to feel rooted in raw ingredients rather than factory precision.

The distressed strokes and loose rhythm mimic ink dragged briskly with a coarse brush across rough, uncoated paper stock. This tactile quality works especially well on small-batch beverage bottles, specialty coffee bags, and seasonal café menus where polished geometric type feels too corporate. Because Hollims maintains standard width proportions and an orderly visual weight beneath its playful slant, titles and flavor descriptions remain distinct across corrugated boxes, tin cans, and mobile promotional campaigns.

Delivered in OTF, TTF, WOFF, and WOFF2 formats, the typeface deploys across packaging die lines, retail signage, and responsive shop graphics. It provides complete character coverage for English, German, Finnish, and Zulu, enabling independent culinary brands to establish a distinct, hand-drawn packaging identity across international markets.
