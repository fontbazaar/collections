Creating child-focused branding or playful packaging often results in types that either lack structure or feel too stiff. DeltaOutlineSSK solves this by combining a chunky geometric skeleton with distinct cutouts and a bouncy retro rhythm. This outline sans serif delivers strong visual impact for headlines and product labels without losing its friendly charm. Across two distinct weights ranging from regular to bold, the letterforms maintain low contrast and normal proportions for high readability in display settings. The family includes both desktop and webfont formats, making it ready for digital and print projects alike. Designers working on branding campaigns can easily set text in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Tagalog, and Indonesian. By leaning into nostalgic seventies geometry, this typeface adds character to any project requiring a lighthearted yet structured voice.
