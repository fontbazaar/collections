Clean vector typography often falls flat when an underground gig poster or skate video title requires raw, physical grit. Authen directly tackles this need with rapid, distressed brush strokes that carry the genuine urgency of wet ink dragged across coarse paper. Developed by the Font Bazaar Team, this display script cuts through sterile digital layouts with ragged edges, offering designers an immediate, hand-drawn texture for loud visual identities.

The two-style family maintains a steady regular weight and balanced, normal proportions, ensuring headline lettering stays punchy and legible against crowded photo backgrounds or gritty halftone screens. Its dry-bristle details capture the fast, unpolished motion native to punk flyers, tour merchandise, and action sports packaging. Rather than forcing you to spend hours building artificial distress through clipping masks, the letterforms introduce bold, tactile friction straight from your keyboard.

Authen ships in OTF, TTF, WOFF, and WOFF2 formats, allowing you to synchronize printed record sleeves and apparel tags with responsive digital graphics. Language support extends to English, Swahili, Somali, Zulu, and Quechua, letting regional music scenes and independent streetwear labels project bold graphic impact across distinct borders. It provides the bite your layouts demand without softening the blow.
