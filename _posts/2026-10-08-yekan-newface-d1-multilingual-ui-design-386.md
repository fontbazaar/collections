Designing interfaces that switch seamlessly between left-to-right and right-to-left scripts often results in jarring typographic mismatches. Yekan NewFace D1 resolves this friction by pairing crisp Latin geometry with meticulously crafted Arabic letterforms, ensuring consistent visual weight across complex layouts. This makes the five-weight family a reliable foundation for software dashboards and mobile applications that must communicate with equal clarity in English, Persian, Arabic, and French.

Built for demanding screen environments, the low-contrast design maintains pristine legibility at small sizes without sacrificing its serious, tech-forward aesthetic. Because it supports a broad range of weights from hairline to bold, designers can establish clear typographic hierarchies in data-dense UI components. The inclusion of OTF, TTF, WOFF, and WOFF2 formats ensures immediate deployment across web and desktop platforms.

Adopting a monoline structure also allows the typeface to perform reliably in corporate branding and logo design, where geometric purity is paramount. By focusing on structural restraint rather than decorative flair, it provides a neutral voice that lets content drive the user experience.
