Designing a wedding invitation that feels genuinely personal rather than digitally printed requires letterforms with the natural irregularity of hand-drawn ink. P22DearestScript provides fine strokes and ornate, sweeping capitals that capture the precise character of historical correspondence. This regular-weight script operates entirely on high contrast, making the sweeping capitals stand out against minimalist layouts without needing artificial resizing. The character set includes support for English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, and Portuguese, ensuring accurate accents for destination stationery and bilingual menus. Available in OTF, TTF, WOFF2, and WOFF formats, the family includes two distinct styles suited for both print production and web-based RSVP forms. By focusing purely on calligraphic authenticity, the script transforms standard romantic branding into tangible heirlooms.
