When architectural signage and industrial branding demand structure that reads from across a concrete hall, standard single-stroke letters often disappear into the background. Elanor Outline solves this distance problem through a striking dual-line construction that maintains geometric precision at large scales without losing legibility. Built for bold headlines, tech-forward posters, and heavy identity work, this all-caps sans serif brings a rigorous, grid-based presence to your compositions.

The single-weight family includes OTF, TTF, WOFF2, and WOFF formats, making it ready for both print installations and digital interfaces. Its robust character set covers major European languages including English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Polish, and Czech. The stark, hollow glyphs command attention entirely through line and negative space, giving modern design systems an uncompromising voice.
