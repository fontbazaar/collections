Crafting a late nineteenth century wanted poster or a frontier apothecary label requires lettering that looks genuinely weathered by the elements rather than digitally distressed. FranciscoAntique solves this exact problem by combining a heavy display serif structure with irregular cutouts and a rugged silhouette. This bold type family channels a distinct Victorian and Western mood, making it well suited for high-impact headlines, dramatic book covers, and tactile product packaging. The release includes three distinct weights ranging from regular to ultra, giving designers precise control over visual density across print and digital mediums. Webfont formats are fully supported alongside standard desktop files, ensuring reliable rendering on modern web pages and promotional sites. Typesetting needs for international projects are covered out of the box with built-in glyph sets for English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Tagalog, and Indonesian. Available now in OTF, TTF, WOFF, and WOFF2 formats, this textured face brings authentic historical grit directly to your design workspace.
