Broadcasting live athletic coverage across international regions demands text that stays legible across fast-moving video feeds, dynamic dashboards, and tight screen constraints. Harir solves this high-velocity design problem through an eight-style humanist sans family that balances rhythmic calligraphic motion with crisp digital clarity. Designed by the Font Bazaar Team, it gives sports networks and app developers a grounded foundation for real-time tickers, match interfaces, and responsive video subtitles.

Its disciplined normal-width architecture and balanced stroke contrast ensure that player statistics, tournament tables, and rapid subtitles remain sharp on varied displays. Ranging from regular to bold weights between 400 and 700, the family stays clean in dense mobile layouts while maintaining an elegant, friendly presence when set as display headers. Specialized figure sets provide dependable horizontal alignment, keeping split times, scoreboards, and kit numbers structured across shifting digital environments.

Because major sporting events cross regional borders, Harir unifies Arabic, Persian, Urdu, Pashto, Kurdish, English, German, and French within a cohesive visual system. Delivered in OTF, TTF, WOFF, and WOFF2 formats, it equips editorial studios and broadcast agencies with a resilient toolkit for sports branding, digital streaming applications, and multilingual publication systems.
