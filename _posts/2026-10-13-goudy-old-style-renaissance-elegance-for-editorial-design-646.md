When an editorial layout or a wedding invitation requires a serious tone rooted in Renaissance tradition, standard digital fonts often feel sterile and modern. Addressing this need for historical warmth, Goudy Old Style brings gentle curved brackets, high-contrast stems, and a distinct vintage mood to long-form body text and luxury packaging labels. This expansive family contains 38 styles spanning regular, bold, and black weights, featuring small caps and diverse figure sets for precise typesetting. Available in OTF, TTF, WOFF2, and WOFF formats, the typeface includes robust language support for English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Tagalog, and Indonesian.
