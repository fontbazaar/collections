Crafting product packaging for artisanal foods or whimsical children goods often leaves designers searching for display lettering that feels tactile rather than digital. Cornish Pasty solves this by introducing hollow double-lined letterforms accented with centered dots inside closed counters. This all-caps sans serif draws heavily from mid-century vintage aesthetics, bringing a distinct charm to food and drink labels, posters, and bold headlines without relying on standard geometric shapes. Because the character set includes support for English and Zulu, branding projects can stretch across diverse markets while maintaining a consistent visual voice.

Built specifically as a single regular weight in open type, true type, and webfont formats, the typeface is ready for both print packaging and online storefronts. The playful outlines demand attention on shelf tags and book covers where personality matters most. Designers working on branding systems that require a handcrafted touch will find that this outlined display face anchors a visual identity with cheerful nostalgia.
