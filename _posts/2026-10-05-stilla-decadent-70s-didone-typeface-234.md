When a record sleeve or a luxury fashion masthead demands a defiant visual anchor, standard geometric sans serifs simply fade into the background. The solution is Stilla, a high-contrast Didone serif that channels a decadent 1970s display aesthetic through exaggerated swelling shapes and surgical hairline incisions. This single-weight black display face commands attention on large-format posters, album packaging, and distinctive logo designs without requiring italics or lighter variants to make its presence felt. Delivered in OTF, TTF, WOFF, and WOFF2 formats, it features robust character sets ready for projects in English, Finnish, Swahili, Zulu, and beyond. Built for designers chasing bold nostalgia and elegant chic, it turns headlines into unavoidable visual statements.
