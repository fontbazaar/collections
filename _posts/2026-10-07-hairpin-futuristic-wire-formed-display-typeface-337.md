When electronic music packaging or stylized sci-fi titles require a distinct physical presence, standard geometric fonts often fall flat, lacking the tactile precision of tangible objects. Hairpin solves this exact problem by mimicking single-stroke bent wire through intricate looped terminals and uncompromising monoline geometry. Built specifically for high-impact branding, posters, and experimental layouts, this eleven-style sans serif family spans multiple visual weights from hairline to bold in normal and extended proportions without any italics. The outline and high-contrast construction delivers a distinctly playful yet cybernetic mood that commands attention in large display settings. Released across OTF, TTF, WOFF, and WOFF2 formats, it includes glyph sets tailored for English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Tagalog, and Indonesian typographic environments. Designers seeking an assertive futuristic aesthetic for album covers and product labels will find its wire-formed aesthetics instantly striking.
