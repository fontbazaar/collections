Designing sports apps, match trackers, and digital scoreboards for Persian-speaking audiences requires strict typographic discipline. Fast-moving data feeds and live telemetry demand absolute clarity under tight spatial constraints, which is why FreeFarsi was drawn with a sturdy, low-contrast sans serif structure. Instead of letting complex calligraphic flourishes crowd dense metric tables, this family focuses on a clean horizontal flow and open counters, ensuring split-second readability on handheld devices.

Created by the Font Bazaar Team in two complementary weights—Regular and Bold—the design bridges structural fidelity with digital performance. It aligns Persian and Arabic letterforms with Latin glyphs, letting product teams build unified interfaces for audiences reading Persian, English, Arabic, or Zulu. Built-in figure sets integrate neatly into score tickers, workout logs, and league leaderboards without disturbing surrounding text baselines.

Available in standard web and desktop packages like WOFF2 and OTF, FreeFarsi supplies the visual punch required for athletic brand marks alongside the quiet stability needed for live commentary feeds. It offers sports UI teams a focused, functional solution where rapid data comprehension is vital.
