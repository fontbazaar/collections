Dense multi-column layouts and lengthy corporate reports often suffer from reader fatigue when the chosen typeface lacks strong vertical stress and compact proportions. To solve this without sacrificing legibility at smaller sizes, CG Times brings sharp bracketed serifs and a structured 400 to 700 weight range to demanding body text. This serious serif family maintains a steady rhythm across complex pages, ensuring that lengthy arguments in academic publishing or financial documentation remain clear and uninterrupted. The six-style family provides the reliable contrast needed for structured hierarchical documents without relying on italic variants. Built for robust cross-border communication, the character set covers English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Turkish, and Polish typography standards out of the box. Available in OTF, TTF, WOFF2, and WOFF formats, the desktop and web fonts integrate directly into professional publishing pipelines and digital platforms. Designers focused on corporate branding and editorial projects will find this transitional design delivers a formal, authoritative voice on both printed paper and high-resolution screens.
