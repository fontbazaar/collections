Large-scale athletic wayfinding and stadium graphics demand uncompromising clarity across vast distances without losing their physical presence. Berthold Imago solves this spatial puzzle through its dense, low-contrast construction rooted in late 1970s and 1980s functional design. Developed by the Font Bazaar Team across 13 upright styles, it delivers a disciplined, serious posture that cuts through visual noise in sports arenas, equipment packaging, and architectural sign systems.

Rather than relying on dramatic geometric quirks, the family draws power from normal-width proportions and firm, stout forms. Ranging from light weights up to black and ultra variants, the letterforms preserve their internal counters even under the heaviest ink loads or low-resolution digital displays. The deliberate lack of italics reinforces its utilitarian focus, prioritizing direct structural weight over decorative speed. Softened terminals temper the rigid geometry just enough to keep dense logotypes approachable on industrial surfaces, posters, and technical apparel.

Shipped in OTF, TTF, WOFF, and WOFF2 formats, Berthold Imago integrates across print and digital identity systems. The family supports Latin-based scripts across languages including German, Tagalog, Spanish, and Indonesian, ensuring coherent execution across international tournament collateral and retail packaging. It offers designers a focused, unapologetic tool for identity programs where authority and pure legibility matter most.
