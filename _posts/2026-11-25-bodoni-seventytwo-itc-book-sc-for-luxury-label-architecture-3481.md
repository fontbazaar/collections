Setting fine print, appellations, and classification lines on high-end packaging often exposes the clunkiness of standard uppercase letters. Bodoni Seventytwo ITC Book SC resolves this tension with a dedicated small-caps cut engineered specifically for balanced, quiet authority. Designed by the Font Bazaar Team, this release channels vintage Victorian discipline through razor-thin hairline serifs and pronounced vertical stress, giving label designers an uncompromising framework for luxury goods.

Rather than scaling down full capitals—which thins out stems and breaks visual weight—this cut retains a true medium stroke thickness across its small-caps glyphs. That consistent density proves essential when composing multi-tier labels for heritage distilleries, fine fragrance bottles, or bespoke apothecary boxes. Paired with proportional figure sets, the high-contrast letterforms keep batch numbers, volume marks, and pedigree details crisp and legible even under meticulous inspection.

Delivered in OTF, TTF, WOFF, and WOFF2 formats, the font fits printed foil stampings as cleanly as responsive e-commerce packaging previews. It includes character support for languages including French, German, Italian, Portuguese, and Tagalog. For projects demanding serious, vintage-tinged refinement where full capitals would overpower delicate package layouts, this single-weight Didone provides measured poise.
