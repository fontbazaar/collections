Building typography for sci-fi video game interfaces, competitive esports graphics, and interactive telemetry displays often leads to a tricky compromise between aggressive cyber styling and legible geometry. Jakone Extended resolves this design tension with broad modular letterforms anchored by smooth, rounded monoline strokes. Developed by the Font Bazaar Team, the family draws on late Y2K techno aesthetics to give headline layouts an authentic electronic signature.

Across its four styles spanning regular and bold weights, the family supplies both extended and condensed proportions that adapt cleanly whether packed into constrained UI banners or stretched across wide promotional posters. Its low-contrast stems and softened contours evoke physical synthesizer panels, tactical HUDs, and electronic music packaging without feeling brittle or overly decorative. Because the letterforms maintain disciplined monoline thickness, titles retain structural clarity against animated backgrounds, detailed 3D assets, and high-contrast dark palettes.

Production files arrive in OTF, TTF, WOFF, and WOFF2 formats to handle print pipelines and responsive web applications. The character set covers Latin-based languages including German, French, Tagalog, and Indonesian, ensuring that esports leagues and digital media campaigns communicate clearly across global player communities. Jakone Extended gives designers a focused technological voice tailored for gaming titles, club flyers, and futuristic brand identities.
