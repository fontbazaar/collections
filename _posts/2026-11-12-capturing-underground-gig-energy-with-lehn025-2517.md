Standard digital typefaces often feel far too sanitized for basement show flyers, punk lineups, and independent record packaging. LEHN025 cuts through that clinical polish with the urgent, spontaneous rhythm of a broad marker drawn directly onto cardstock. Designed by Font Bazaar Team in a single regular weight, this handwritten display face brings an immediate punch of human chaos that resists clean alignment.

The visual construction leans into a grungy, playful attitude defined by low-contrast, medium-weight strokes that stay readable even through photocopier degradation or screen-printed fabric. It is built specifically for headline-scale applications, music flyers, album labels, and streetwear emblems that demand tactile grit instead of crisp geometry. Because each character carries the raw momentum of rapid scribbling, titles set in LEHN025 project genuine tension without relying on artificial distressing filters.

For international tour schedules and cross-border releases, the character set covers languages including Spanish, German, Portuguese, and Tagalog. Delivered in OTF, TTF, WOFF, and WOFF2 formats, LEHN025 provides an unvarnished, physical voice ready for poster runs, cassette sleeves, and digital flyers alike.
