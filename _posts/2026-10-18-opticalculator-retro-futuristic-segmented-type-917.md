Standard grotesks lack the unmistakable glow of vintage phosphor screens, while generic bitmap fonts often crumble at display sizes. Enter OPTICalculator, a slanted seven-segment typeface that translates the rigid geometry of classic electronic calculators into a sharp, all-caps design. Built with low contrast and normal width proportions, this regular weight pixel display font anchors sci-fi headlines and web UI elements in an authentic retro-futuristic mood.

Crafted specifically for bold display applications, gaming themes, and tech-heavy branding, the family includes two distinct styles supplied in OTF, TTF, WOFF2, and WOFF formats with webfont ready technical features.
