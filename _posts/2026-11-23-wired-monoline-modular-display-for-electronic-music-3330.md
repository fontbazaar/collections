Designing physical record sleeves and digital assets for electronic music often runs into a wall when technical display faces feel too chaotic to hold a coherent layout. Wired solves this tension by locking boxy modular geometry into a calm monoline framework that channels late-nineties cyber aesthetics without sacrificing typographic control. Its extended proportions and rounded rectangular counters bring a steady mechanical rhythm to vinyl packaging, tracklists, and club identity systems.

Developed by the Font Bazaar Team, the family spans four weights ranging from Light through Black and Ultra. The horizontal stretch and low-contrast strokes keep headlines punchy and assertive across gig posters, cassette labels, and hardware faceplates. Because the interior counters echo the logic of electrical circuits and synthesizer panels, titles establish a distinctly synthetic atmosphere that anchors an entire release.

Beyond heavy display titles on physical sleeves, the lighter weights adapt cleanly to compact track metadata, streaming banners, and gaming interfaces. Available in OTF, TTF, WOFF, and WOFF2 formats, Wired includes character sets tailored for projects in German, Spanish, Tagalog, and Indonesian. It gives designers an unapologetically synthetic voice that stays crisp across high-density print and digital screens.
