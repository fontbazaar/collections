Designing bilingual editorial layouts often forces a compromise when standard geometric types fail to capture the nuanced rhythm of Middle Eastern scripts. W_larzeh resolves this tension by introducing high contrast and dynamic letterforms rooted in traditional calligraphic movement, yet restrained enough to function as a contemporary sans serif. This regular weight brings a distinct personality to cultural posters and logotypes without relying on ornamental excess.

Built specifically for Arabic and Persian typography, the single-style family relies on sweeping baseline shifts and sharp terminals to create striking titles. Because the visual weight is balanced for display work, it holds the page with authority in magazine headers and packaging.

Available in OTF, TTF, WOFF, and WOFF2 formats, the desktop and web licenses integrate cleanly into digital and print workflows. It offers a direct solution for designers seeking authentic calligraphic character within modern branding systems.
