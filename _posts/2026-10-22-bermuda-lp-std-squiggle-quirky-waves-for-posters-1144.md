When a festival poster or a craft beverage label needs to break away from sterile geometric grids, standard type options often fall flat. Bermuda LP Std Squiggle solves this by injecting an immediate handcrafted texture into heavy letterforms. Packed with quirky vertical waves inside bold outlines, this all-caps sans serif demands attention without relying on traditional geometric precision. It delivers a distinctly playful mood that suits loud headlines and memorable product packaging.

Built across two styles ranging from regular to bold visual weights, the family adapts easily to tight display spaces. Designers working on event flyers or snack packaging will find its outlined structure cuts through busy backgrounds cleanly. The font ships in OTF, TTF, WOFF2, and WOFF formats, ensuring reliable rendering across print and web projects.

Multilingual typesetting runs smoothly across major European and regional tongues, covering English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Tagalog, and Indonesian out of the box. Without italics to clutter the family, the focus stays squarely on the bouncy, wave-filled stems. Use it when your next branding project or bold poster design calls for something genuinely animated.
