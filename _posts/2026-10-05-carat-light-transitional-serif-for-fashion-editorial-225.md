When luxury packaging demands a delicate touch that still commands attention at small sizes, standard geometric sans faces often fall flat. Addressing this exact tension in high-end editorial and branding projects, the light transitional serif known as Carat introduces sharp contrast and refined bracketed serifs that bring distinct personality to high-end labels. Designed with a normal width and a weight of 300, the single-style family relies on dramatic stroke variation to create striking visual rhythms across magazine spreads and cosmetic boxes. The desktop and web-ready formats ensure consistent rendering across print and digital touchpoints without losing fidelity in the hairline terminals. Built for international design teams, the character set includes complete support for English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Turkish, and Polish copy. Available in OTF, TTF, WOFF, and WOFF2 formats, this typeface provides a precise typographic solution for projects centered on fashion, cosmetics, and curated retail branding.
