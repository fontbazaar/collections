When creating physical product packaging that needs to stand out from across a crowded room, standard geometric sans faces often lack the necessary grit and industrial presence. To solve this visual challenge, the stenciled display typeface Block introduces prominent interior cutouts and chunky geometric forms rooted in retro poster traditions. Across nine distinct styles spanning multiple widths and weights from regular to bold, this release delivers robust utility for gritty editorial layouts and product labels without relying on decorative flourishes. Designers working on multilingual projects can set copy in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Tagalog, and Indonesian using the included OTF, TTF, WOFF2, and WOFF font files, ensuring consistent rendering from desktop print workflows to webfont ready digital applications.
