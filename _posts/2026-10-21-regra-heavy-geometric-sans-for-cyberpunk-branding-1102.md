Designing identity systems for competitive esports teams or gritty sci-fi interfaces requires letterforms that convey raw power without losing structural clarity. Regra solves this by merging rigid geometric proportions with sharp angular cuts that demand attention at large scales. Available in three distinct weights spanning from regular to black, this neo-grotesque sans serif anchors aggressive branding, posters, and product packaging with unapologetic visual dominance. Built with low contrast and sturdy strokes, it maintains absolute legibility across dense layouts while projecting a distinctly 90s cyberpunk aesthetic. The complete package includes standard OTF, TTF, WOFF, and WOFF2 formats, ensuring robust performance across print deliverables and web interfaces. Designers working on international projects can immediately deploy the typeface across English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Tagalog, and Indonesian markets without missing a glyph.
