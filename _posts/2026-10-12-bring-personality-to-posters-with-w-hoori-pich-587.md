When a packaging design or poster needs a break from rigid geometry, standard sans serifs often fall flat and fail to capture attention. W_hoori pich solves this by introducing ornate swirled terminals and a lighthearted, hand-drawn rhythm that injects distinct personality into headings and book covers. This single-weight regular script carries a charming informality that works particularly well for bold logo projects and product labels requiring a human touch rather than mechanical perfection. Built to handle diverse typographic landscapes, the character set includes full support for Arabic and Persian scripts, allowing designers to maintain a consistent decorative voice across multilingual layouts. Delivered in OTF, TTF, WOFF, and WOFF2 formats, it integrates smoothly into both print workflows and web projects where display typography needs to stand out through unique ornamental detail.
