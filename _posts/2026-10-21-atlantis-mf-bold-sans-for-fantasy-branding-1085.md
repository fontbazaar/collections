Building a brand identity for a modern fantasy tabletop game or designing a speculative fiction book cover often requires letterforms that avoid standard corporate sterility while steering clear of worn-out medieval tropes. Atlantis MF solves this through striking structural geometry paired with unorthodox horizontal wedge terminals that immediately demand attention in large-scale layouts. The regular visual weight and low contrast construction keep titles legible even when applied across packaging and labels, while the all caps layout establishes a commanding presence on posters and flyers. This display face operates at the intersection of a bold and strong posture with a genuinely playful rhythm, suiting adventurous projects that need an enigmatic edge. Designers working in English, German, Spanish, or Portuguese can immediately implement the character set across multilingual campaigns using the included OTF, TTF, WOFF2, and WOFF file formats.
