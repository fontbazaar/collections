Designing packaging for a dark craft beer or a fantasy tabletop roleplaying book often requires a typeface that avoids modern sterility and embraces historical grit. Wittenberger Frakt MT solves this challenge with sharply chiseled vertical strokes, intricate uppercase forms, and high contrast that instantly evoke medieval manuscripts and gothic printing presses. This traditional blackletter brings serious, mysterious weight to editorial headlines, product labels, and specialized branding without leaning into cartoonish horror clichés. Available in regular and bold weights with two distinct styles, the family covers essential characters for typesetting in english, german, french, spanish, and italian across modern OTF, TTF, WOFF, and WOFF2 formats. Designers crafting historical book covers or moody packaging will find that these calligraphic strokes demand attention through bold presence and nostalgic texture.
