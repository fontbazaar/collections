Designing a high-end cultural magazine layout often requires striking a delicate balance between legible body text and bold headlines that demand attention. Goodfish addresses this challenge through pronounced wedge terminals and a high contrast axis that anchors both multi-column articles and oversized cover text. Spanning eight styles across regular and bold visual weights without italics, the family maintains a consistent normal width that holds the page with quiet authority.

Built for branding projects, packaging labels, and sophisticated editorial spreads, this serif adapts across diverse applications while keeping its sharp distinctive terminals intact. The character set accommodates English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Turkish, and Polish copy requirements.

Provided in OTF, TTF, WOFF2, and WOFF formats, the desktop and web font files integrate directly into your production pipeline for print and digital publishing environments.
