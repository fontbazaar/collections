When a magazine layout or a luxury packaging project demands visual tension, standard typefaces often fall flat in the details. Fenice solves this by pushing stroke contrast to absolute extremes, creating a sharp, sculptural rhythm across twenty-one styles ranging from light to black and ultra. This high-contrast serif makes a powerful statement in large headlines and bold logos, while retaining surprising legibility for structured body text when set with care.

Built for demanding design environments, the family includes OTF, TTF, WOFF2, and WOFF formats to cover both print production and digital interfaces. The character set handles complex typesetting across English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Tagalog, and Indonesian without missing a beat.

By committing fully to its bold weight range and Didone roots, this release avoids timid middle grounds. It provides designers with a robust toolkit for building sophisticated brand identities and packaging systems that require both elegance and uncompromising structural impact.
