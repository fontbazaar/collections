Capturing genuine psychological dread on a movie poster requires typography that feels grounded yet fundamentally corrupted. akoom answers that demand by grafting the precision of architectural blueprints onto cracked, weathered serif letterforms. Instead of relying on clean modernism or predictable splatter effects, this family introduces an unsettling industrial decay that roots your titles in forgotten archives, abandoned structures, and forensic schematics.

Built specifically for commanding headlines, posters, and flyers, the release includes two styles across regular and bold visual presences. Each glyph combines traditional serif proportions with distressed textures, technical draft lines, and weathered ink bleeds. The letterforms hold up with terrifying clarity at massive scale, giving designers the tactile grit required for psychological thrillers, gothic book jackets, and moody editorial spreads.

The typeface supports English language typesetting and comes packaged in OTF, TTF, WOFF, and WOFF2 formats to handle both physical print runs and screen titles. When your composition demands calculated terror rather than decorative chaos, akoom establishes a cold, clinical atmosphere that stays etched into the paper.
